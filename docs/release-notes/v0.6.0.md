# xrFPS 0.6.0

Frame timing: a full-window view of every frame a VR game renders, measured
on your real headset. Where the HUD tells you *that* a game is missing
frames, frame timing tells you *which* frames, *by how much*, and *why*.

## New

- **Frame timing.** Press **Open frame timing** on the Control Center's
  Dashboard while a game is running. The viewer shows:
  - delivered FPS, frame-time percentiles, 1% lows, hitches and headroom;
  - a live frame-time graph with GPU time overlaid, over the last 5 s to 5 min;
  - a distribution of frame times;
  - every late frame with its likely cause: the app's CPU work, the GPU, or
    the runtime.

  Click any frame for its breakdown:
  - the app's own work;
  - the runtime's pacing wait in `xrWaitFrame`;
  - time the runtime held the app in `xrBeginFrame`;
  - xrFPS's HUD work and the runtime's submission in `xrEndFrame`;
  - the frame's GPU time.

  **Record** saves a capture under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\xrFPS\captures`.

- **Measured inside the game, on any runtime.** The layer timestamps every
  `xrWaitFrame`, `xrBeginFrame` and `xrEndFrame` call the game makes and
  publishes them to shared memory. It needs no runtime support, so it works
  on Meta (Quest over Link or Air Link), SteamVR and any other OpenXR runtime.

- **GPU time from the game's own queue.**
  - D3D12 and OpenGL: timestamps on the CPU clock.
  - Vulkan: timestamps on the CPU clock where the driver offers calibrated
    timestamps.
  - D3D11: durations only.

  The **GPU timing** box in Readouts → Frame statistics turns this off.
  `XRFPS_GPU_TIMING=0/1` pins it for one game, and `XRFPS_FRAME_TIMELINE=0`
  turns frame timing off for one game.

- **A more useful log.** Every line of `xrfps.log` now carries the time and
  the game's process id. The log also records where the HUD was placed
  relative to your head, and why a HUD you summoned was left out (no free
  layer, no swapchain, upload failed) or refused by the runtime. If the HUD
  ever fails to appear, **Open log** now says why.

## Install or update

Use *Check for updates* inside the Control Center, or download and run
`xrFPS-0.6.0-Setup.exe`, which upgrades an existing install in place and
refreshes the layer; games started afterwards load the new one. The
installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen shows a warning: choose
*More info → Run anyway*, or verify your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
